Author's note: this blog is going to be more personal than the previous ones.

First of all, the reason why I quit writing this blog was because I cheched GitHub's stadistics and I realized no one read it, so I just stopped. Even now, it feels weird to apologize when in theory no one is reading; but even then, if you happen to be reading this, thanks.

During this last months I've been doing okey. Some weeks have been better than others, but mostly I've been relaxed in part thanks to having started HRT, and in great part thanks to my wonderful girlfriend. But even while calm, I felt like I wasn't being productive, that I was only consuming and had stopped creating.

For the last weeks I've been trying to get back to creating, to get back to my hobbies one by one. I've upgraded the sound engine of my gameboy game, I've gone back to playing piano and guitar, and I even fixed my laptop screen.

This monday I was really bored, so I decided to pick up my japanese cart of Pokemon Silver, which I bought for cheap off from ebay and only had to replace the save battery. I only had one problem when playing, I don't really speak japanese. I studied a bit a long time ago, but didn't really get to a good level. So to make things a bit easier for me, I made a little hiragana/katakana chart to consult while playing.

I cut an index card and it ended up with the perfect size for a 10x5 grid of 1cm x 1cm squares. I wrote the hiragana chart on one side, and the katakana on the other with a pen. I punched some holes into the card to pass a string to hang it from the cartdridge.

So I've been playing Pokemon Silver without fully understanding the dialogues. I can google the meaning of common words so I've been learning vocabulary, and on jisho.org I can search for the kanji meaning and how to write them (not sponsored).

At the time of wrtiting I've completed the first gym and the Bellsprout Tower, but still haven't hatched Togepi.
