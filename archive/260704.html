I've been reading a lot of manga over the last week. Most of it was yuri (the japanese term for lesbian media).

I started by reading The moon under a rainy night, and finished all that was available in 2 days. I wanted to see the anime, but it hasn't been released. I also looked at other works by the same author, but I find that one to be the cuttest of them all. It's about a girl who falls for another girl who has problems hearing, and the manga is full of diverse characters with different backstories. I find it to be similar to Love me for who I am, in the ways that it treats the various character's situations.

Then I obsessed over Welcome to the Ghost Mansion, which tecnically isn't yuri but the protagonists do be roomates, A girl who struggles to find tennants for her haunted building, so she shifts her focus and starts renting for the spectres. It is a really funny read, and the core principle of it is to just talk things out.

Also I have to mention the Green yuri (The guy she was interested in wasn't a guy at all). Mostly because is one of the best mangas to ever come out and be available in many regions, and partly to have it as the photo for this entry. By the way, there are plans for an anime release, so stay tunned ;3.

Finally I revisited some of Suzuki senpai's works, some a little safer for work than others. But I still haven't found a job, so I didn't care.
