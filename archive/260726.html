I had been experimenting with a watercolor brush on Krita, but I felt something was missing. It didn't feel the same as it looked when I watched someone paint with real watercolors, my curiosity for the real thing grew.

I bought a cheap watercolor kit online, it came with a brush and some watercolor paper which aparently is really important. It also came with a pencil, so I started by doing a sketch of a flower to "fill in" later. When deciding the colors to use, I made use of a sheet which I think was ment for that but I'm not sure since I can't read Chinese. I discovered all of the subtleties that come with painting with real materials, similar to when I use a cheap pen to draw.

The next day I wanted to experiment a bit more, so I decided to redraw some images from the manual of Painter Momopie. I discovered that depending on the amounts of water and pigment the resulting tone and texture varied, and with that I could do shadows without changing colors.

witch watercolor painting (scan)

I'm having fun with the watercolors, learning the different ways they can be used. There's so much variance that can be achieved, and I don't think it could be replicated in software without expensive equipment. Now I think I'll have to learn color theory.
